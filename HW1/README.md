**HW1 - Bass Model: Innovation Diffusion Analysis**



**Course**: DS 223 Marketing Analytics  

**Selected Innovation:** Garmin Index Sleep Monitor  

**Historical Look-Alike:** Fitbit Fitness Trackers



**Project Overview**



For this homework, I chose the Garmin Index Sleep Monitor from TIME's Best Inventions of 2025. Since it is a new product, I used Fitbit's historical sales data from 2010 to 2019 to understand how similar technology spread among consumers.

First, I used Fitbit's sales from 2010 to 2016 to estimate the Bass Model parameters p, q, and M. I also compared the model's predictions with the actual sales from 2017 to 2019 to see how well it worked.

After that, I used the estimated parameters to predict Garmin's adoption over 15 years. Since I could not find the exact market potential for the Garmin Index Sleep Monitor, I used Fermi logic and considered three scenarios: 186000, 372000, and 930000 devices. These are my assumptions, not actual Garmin sales.
